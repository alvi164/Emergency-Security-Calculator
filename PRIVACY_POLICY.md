# Privacy Policy for ES Calculator

**Effective Date: October 26, 2026**

ES Calculator ("we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

## 1. Information Collection and Use

### 1.1 Camera and Microphone
ES Calculator accesses your device's camera and microphone to record video and audio evidence in emergency situations triggered by the user (e.g., triple-shake). These recordings are stored locally on your device.

### 1.2 Location Information
We may collect and process information about your actual location (GPS coordinates) when a recording is triggered. This information is used to provide context to the recorded evidence and is stored alongside the video files.

### 1.3 Storage
The application requires access to your device's internal storage to save the recorded evidence. These files are stored in a private directory accessible only by the application.

## 2. Data Sharing and Disclosure

### 2.1 Local Storage
All recorded evidence (video, audio, and location data) is stored locally on your device. We do not have access to this data unless you explicitly choose to upload it to your own configured cloud storage (e.g., Firebase).

### 2.2 Third-Party Services
If you choose to enable cloud backup, your data will be transmitted to Google Firebase. Please review Google's Privacy Policy for information on how they handle data. We do not share your information with any other third parties.

## 3. Security
We take the security of your data seriously. We use industry-standard security measures to protect the information stored within the app. However, please remember that no method of transmission over the internet or method of electronic storage is 100% secure.

## 4. User Controls
You have full control over the application's permissions. You can enable or disable camera, microphone, and location access at any time through your device's system settings.

## 5. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
